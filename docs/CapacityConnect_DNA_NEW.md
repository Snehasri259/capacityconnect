# 1. THE PRODUCT IN ONE SIMPLE SENTENCE

Capacity Connect helps an organization understand:

**What a person needs to know → what they currently know → what they are missing → what they should learn → who can teach them → whether they actually improved.**

It is not just an LMS.

It is a:

**Competency-Driven Digital Capacity Intelligence Platform.**

---

# 2. THE CORE PROBLEM

Traditional training systems mainly answer:

* What courses are available?
* Who enrolled?
* Who completed the course?
* What was their score?

Capacity Connect should answer much more:

* What skills are required for a role?
* What is the trainee's current competency?
* What skills are missing?
* How serious is each skill gap?
* What should the trainee learn first?
* Which course is most useful?
* Which trainer is most suitable?
* Is the trainee actually improving?
* Does the trainer need to intervene?
* Did the intervention work?
* How is the organization's overall capability changing?

This is the heart of the project.

# 4. PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

The entire platform follows:

**REQUIRE → MEASURE → IDENTIFY → RECOMMEND → LEARN → ASSESS → INTERVENE → REASSESS → IMPROVE**

Every major feature should support one or more steps of this process.

Do not build disconnected features.

# 15. SKILL GAP ENGINE

The platform compares:

**Required competency − Current competency**

Example:

Required:

80

Current:

55

Gap:

25

Then classify the gap.

Example configurable system:

* 0–10 = Strong
* 11–25 = Minor
* 26–50 = Moderate
* 51–75 = High
* 76–100 = Critical

The system should also consider:

* Importance of the competency
* Role requirement
* Assessment confidence
* Previous performance
* Practical evidence
* Trainer feedback

# 45. ROUTE PROTECTION

Every application route must check:

1. Is the user authenticated?
2. Is the account active?
3. What is the user's role?
4. Does the role have permission?
5. Does the user own or have access to this resource?

Example:

A trainee must never access:

`/admin/users`

Even if they manually type the URL.

Backend authorization must also enforce this.

Frontend route protection alone is NOT security.
